Data adapted from TFC Structure Compat
======================================

Part of this pack's structure data is adapted from TFC Structure Compat by EasyShotzz (an expanded fork of
claustra01's "TFC x YUNG's Better Series"; the mod's metadata declares the MIT license), which the pack also uses
as a mod (mods/tfc-structure.pw.toml).

What is adapted:

- kubejs/data/mvs/worldgen/structure/*.json are the mod's overrides of Moog's Voyager Structures' structure
  definitions (spawn rules for TFC), with one change each: the `biomes` field names a tag under
  `pack:tfc_structures/` instead of one of the `c:is_*` tags, so that the pack can keep TFC's biomes out of those
  shared tags (docs/design.md, "Structure mods"). They are written by tools/structure_mods/biome_tags.py from the
  mod's own files.
- kubejs/data/pack/tags/worldgen/biome/tfc_structures/*.json hold the biome lists the mod puts into the `c:is_*`
  tags.

The mod's idea of rewriting a structure's blocks to TFC blocks is the model for `pack_world`'s structure skin
(mod/src/main/java/pack/world/skin/), which is the pack's own code. Dynamic TFC Structures (SH42913/tfc_structures,
MIT) took the same route through a mixin into StructureStart.placeInChunk; no code was copied from either mod.
