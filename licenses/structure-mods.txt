Structure mods and their companions: licences and terms
========================================================

None of these mods is bundled in the pack: packwiz stores a download link and a hash for each (mods/*.pw.toml), and the
launcher fetches the author's own file. The pack's own code and data that work with them (mod/, kubejs/data/pack/pack_world/,
tools/structure_mods/, tools/loot/, the villager trade table) are the pack's. Licences are as the authors state them on Modrinth
or CurseForge and in the jars (not legal advice); where the two disagree both are given.
